Know who’s on which job, where the tools are, and when the truck is due.

CrewFlo is the operations app for construction, agriculture, and field service teams. Jobs, tasks, equipment, parts, and maintenance live in one place your crew can actually keep up to date.

On Android through Google Play, and in any browser for the office.

One app for the office and the field

Managers plan the work and see where it stands. Crews see what’s theirs, start it, and log what they used. Everyone works from the same records.

Plan jobs around the pay period

Set up jobs and sub-jobs, attach the crews and equipment they need, and watch labor hours and task progress fill in as the pay period runs.

  • Active, inactive, and template jobs in one place
  • Crews, equipment, and vehicles attached to each job
  • Labor hours and task progress for the pay period

Write down how the work gets done, once

Templates hold the steps, priority, and time for the work you repeat, from floor prep to the final coat. Assign them to a job and each person gets their tasks for the pay period, with a Start button right on the card.

  • Reusable task templates and team bundles
  • Each person sees their own list; managers can see everyone’s
  • The know-how stays when someone new starts or someone leaves

Every machine keeps its own history

Each vehicle and piece of equipment carries its repairs, scheduled maintenance, time on jobs, and what it has cost you. Recurring service comes due on its own schedule, and a repair that replaces the same part counts as the service.

  • Flag a repair right from the asset
  • Recurring maintenance assigned to a person and a pay period
  • Cost history that stays put when you change how you work

Know what’s on the shelf before you need it

Track parts and consumables by location, from lumber on the tool trailer to oil by the gallon. Low stock gets flagged, and crews can request what they need from the field.

  • Counted items and stock measured by volume
  • Low-stock flags so you reorder in time
  • Part requests that the shop can fill and track

Hands full? Say it instead.

Tap the mic and say what happened. CrewFlo drafts the task, repair, or equipment record for you to check and confirm. Ask what’s open today or which tools are checked out, and it answers out loud.

  • Knows your crew, equipment, and tool names
  • Nothing is saved until you confirm it
  • Spoken answers for hands-free use on site
